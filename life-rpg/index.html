🔁 Dailies

Reset every day. A miss at rollover costs HP — but Dailies never get deadline-locked. Tap Complete as many times as you do it in a day (multiple dog walks count): each tap earns its reward, your streak counts days, not taps.

📅 Weekly Challenges

A minimum to hit each week, Monday–Sunday. Tap Log each time you do it. Each log earns +10 XP / +10 gold; hitting the weekly target pays +50 gold, +25 XP. No HP penalty for missing a week — it's a target, not a daily.
